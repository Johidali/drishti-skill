1. Problem Statement & Root Cause Analysis
Slide 2 / 7

Vulnerabilities in Manual & Passive Audits

  • Ghost Trainees: Biometrics or paper records are punched during morning visits, while classrooms remain empty during scheduled sessions.
  • Equipment Hopping: Expensive machinery (lathes, sewing sets, IT equipment) is borrowed or rented temporarily solely to pass scheduled physical visits.
  • Dormant CCTV Hardware: In-place cameras record passive footage to local storage without automated integrity checks.

Why Traditional Surveillance Solutions Fail

  • Rural Bandwidth Choke: Cloud video streaming (RTSP) demands 2.5 Mbps per camera, exhausting rural data limits in under 48 hours.
  • Privacy Violations: Mass biometric scanning and continuous facial identification violate the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act.
  • High Recurring Cost: Continuous cloud compute for millions of video hours makes central scaling financially unviable.

3. Technical Architecture & Low-Bandwidth Pipeline
Slide 4 / 7

Edge-to-Cloud Pipeline Execution

  • 1. Capture: Pulls frames from existing IP/RTSP camera feeds.
  • 2. Inference: Computes aggregated headcounts & validates assets.
  • 3. Masking: Blurs facial bounding boxes directly in memory.
  • 4. Discrepancy Rules: Checks: $\Delta \text{Headcount} > 20\%$ or missing machinery.
  • 5. Micro-Push: Dispatches MQTT/REST telemetry to MSDE portal.

Mitigating Edge Cases

  • Crowd Occlusion: Uses head-density estimation to detect seated trainees when lower bodies are hidden by desks.
  • Temporary Exits: A 15-minute moving average window prevents false alerts when trainees take short breaks.
  • Low Illumination: Automatic contrast-limited adaptive histogram equalization (CLAHE) cleans dark classroom footage.
